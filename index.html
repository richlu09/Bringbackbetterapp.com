Our mission

Bring Back Better is a community-first movement built on kindness, respect, honesty, accountability, family, and connection. Everyone age 16 or older is welcome, with no upper age limit. We believe every generation has something valuable to offer.

We're starting locally, with the goal of growing into communities across the country.

What brings us together

Kindness & respect

Encouraging thoughtful conversations, good deeds, and treating people with dignity.

Across generations

Connecting neighbors to share experiences, skills, stories, and wisdom.

Community connections

Making room for local ideas, participation, and neighbors supporting neighbors.

Community events

Our vision includes a place where community members can share local events and activities. As Bring Back Better grows, we also hope to host events that bring people together in person.
